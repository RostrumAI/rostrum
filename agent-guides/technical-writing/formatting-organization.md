# Formatting and organization

Use this guidance for examples, figures, images, footnotes, notices, and other document structures not covered by the Markdown, accessibility, or procedure guides.

## Examples

Use examples to clarify a rule or demonstrate a realistic task. Keep examples subordinate to the product definition. Introduce a short example with `such as` or `like`, or use `For example` in a separate sentence for a longer example. Introduce a standalone sample, output block, image, or table with a complete sentence.

## Figures and images

Use an image only when it explains something that text cannot explain efficiently. Do not use images for text, code, terminal output, or information that exists only in the image. Introduce informative images with a complete sentence, provide concise contextual alt text, and put complex details in surrounding text. Use an empty `alt=""` for decorative images or images that duplicate nearby text. Prefer SVG for diagrams when practical. Crop screenshots to relevant content and remove or cover PII.

See `accessibility.md` for keyboard access, semantic markup, captions, and nonvisual meaning.

## Footnotes

Use footnotes only when they provide useful supporting information without interrupting the main task. Prefer a direct sentence, a parenthetical clarification, or a selective link when that is clearer. Keep footnotes concise, place them consistently, and do not put required instructions or essential definitions only in a footnote.

## Notes and notices

Use a note, warning, or other notice only when the information changes how the reader should proceed or prevents a meaningful error. Introduce the notice with a concise, descriptive label. Do not use notices for ordinary prose, decoration, or information that belongs in the procedure itself.

## Audio, video, and animation

Provide captions, transcripts, or descriptions for audio and video. Do not use flashing or flickering content. Do not use animated GIFs when a more efficient accessible format works.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Examples are realistic, minimal, and clearly subordinate to the product definition.
- [ ] Images add information that text cannot communicate as efficiently.
- [ ] Images have appropriate alt text and surrounding context.
- [ ] Footnotes are optional supporting material, not hidden requirements.
- [ ] Notices are necessary, labeled, and actionable.
- [ ] Multimedia has captions, transcripts, or descriptions.
